Get Involved

Three ways to join the work.

Whether you organize, research, advocate, or fund — there's a place for you in this coalition. Pacific Islanders for Progress is stronger when our community moves together.

01 — Join the Coalition

Become part of a statewide network.

PIP's coalition connects organizations and advocates across California into a shared agenda for Pacific Islander communities. When we move together, decision-makers take notice.

  • Access to disaggregated data, research, and policy briefs
  • Coordinated advocacy campaigns and shared messaging
  • Invitations to coalition convenings and community events
  • Amplification of your organization's work through PIP's network
  • Connection to other Pacific Islander-serving organizations statewide
  • Talanoa-grounded relationship building across communities

Ready to join?

Join our Google Group to connect with coalition members, receive updates, and participate in shared advocacy. Open to organizations and individuals committed to Pacific Islander communities in California.
